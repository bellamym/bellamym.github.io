OM 855 — Project Management (MBA)
Spring 2026
Strategic dimensions of project management including project selection, definition, planning, execution, and monitoring. Applies quantitative tools to evaluate project performance; includes case readings and team deliverables for a real project of choice.

Spring 2026
Tools, techniques, roles, and responsibilities critical to managing programs and projects. Explores management at the operational and strategic level and evaluates performance across a portfolio of projects at the business unit or firm level.
